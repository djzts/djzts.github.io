# Zhongqi (Thomas) Zhao, Ph.D.

**Zhongqi (Thomas) Zhao** is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Brookhaven National Laboratory. The research brings quantum computing to power and energy systems, developing hybrid quantum-classical optimization methods and applying them to grid operation, data center energy management, and distribution network analytics.

The work sits at the interface of quantum computing, mathematical optimization, and power engineering. Current projects at Brookhaven study quantum computing for AC optimal power flow and build machine learning data pipelines for underground power-grid fault location with Con Edison.

## Research Interests

- Quantum computing and hybrid quantum-classical optimization
- Mixed-integer programming and decomposition methods (Benders' decomposition, Lagrangian relaxation)
- Quantum annealing and the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA)
- Power system optimization, including AC optimal power flow and unit commitment
- Data center and energy system management
- Distribution grid analytics: PV hosting capacity, FLISR, protection device placement, fault location

## Research Vision

Large-scale power and energy problems are full of discrete decisions that strain classical solvers. Zhongqi's research asks where today's quantum hardware can genuinely help: splitting hard mixed-integer problems so that quantum annealers or gate-based circuits handle the combinatorial core while classical solvers handle the rest. The goal is practical, open-source tooling that grid operators and energy researchers can use as quantum hardware matures.

## Academic Training

Zhongqi received a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Houston in 2025, advised by Dr. Lei Fan and Dr. Zhu Han, with the dissertation *Hybrid Quantum-Classical Optimization for Data Center Energy System*. Before that, Zhongqi earned an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Minnesota - Twin Cities (2020) and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics through a joint program between the University of Minnesota - Twin Cities and Beijing Jiaotong University (2018).

Industry and national-lab experience includes a graduate internship at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (2023), modeling spatial-temporal PV hosting capacity with battery storage for the San Francisco grid, and a PSS/E software developer internship at Siemens (2025), building sparse matrix computation modules in Fortran 90.

## Selected Research Themes

**Hybrid Quantum-Classical Benders' Decomposition**  
Multi-cut and QAOA-assisted Benders' decomposition for mixed-integer linear programming, run on both quantum annealers and digital quantum computers, and released as the open-source [HQC-Bend](https://github.com/djzts/HQCMCBD-API) package.

**Data Center Energy Management**  
Co-optimizing energy supply and IT service scheduling for internet data centers, including emergency operation.

**Distribution Grid Analytics**  
PV hosting capacity estimation, self-healing FLISR analysis and recloser/fuse placement for SAIFI reduction with Entergy Texas, and fault-location data pipelines with Con Edison.
